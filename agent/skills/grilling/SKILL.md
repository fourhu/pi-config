name: grilling
description: Relentless questions on a plan, decision, or idea until there is an explicit consensus.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

## Step 1 — Walk the decision tree

Advance one branch at a time. One question, wait for the answer, then the next.

**Done when:** every branch has been visited and answered.

## Step 2 — Facts vs decisions

- Facts the workspace or tools can answer → look them up. Do not ask.
- Judgement and trade-offs → ask the user and wait.

**Done when:** every open item is either looked up or answered.

## Reference

Do not act further until the user explicitly confirms consensus.
