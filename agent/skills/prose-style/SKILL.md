# Prose

For technical readers.

- Direct and restrained. Internal wiki, not a keynote.
- No stock transitions: "as we all know", "needless to say", "it is worth noting", "let us dive in".
- No flattery: "hope this helps", "feel free to comment".
- No slogan labels: "shell vs soul", "two layers of voice".
- "Note", "in other words", "by contrast" are allowed; use them sparingly.
- Quote sources instead of paraphrasing when the reader should verify.
- Claims need a concrete example or snippet. No free-floating abstraction.
